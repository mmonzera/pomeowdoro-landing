Get Pomeowdoro

A tiny 1 MB installer. It sets itself up for your account only, no admin rights needed.

Download for Windows (v2.0)
1. DownloadSave Pomeowdoro-setup.exe.
2. InstallRun it and click through. Pomeowdoro starts and the pill appears at the top of your screen.
3. SmartScreen?Click "More info" then "Run anyway". The app isn't code-signed yet.
Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tasks and settings stay on your own computer.

Does it use the internet?

Only if you switch on synced lyrics in Settings. Then it sends the song title and artist to lrclib.net to find the lyrics. Otherwise it never connects to anything.

How do I close it?

Open the panel, go to Settings and press "Quit Pomeowdoro". You can also turn on "Launch at login" there.

It covers part of my browser tabs.

Settings has a Size option and a "Shrink to a thin line when idle" switch. The window is only as big as the pill, so the rest of your screen stays clickable.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Pomeowdoro is a Windows 10 / 11 app for now.

Why does Windows warn me?

The app is new and not code-signed, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
