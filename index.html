Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no tracking. Your settings are stored on your own computer.

Why does Windows warn me?

The app is new and not code-signed, so SmartScreen shows a warning. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".

Can I change the 25 minutes?

Yes. Click the big digits on the screen while the timer is stopped and type any length, like 30 or 12:30.
