Pomeowdoro

A tiny retro Pomodoro timer with a pixel cat. Your cursor becomes its toy while you get things done.

Live demo: hit START, then move your mouse over the green screen

Small app, big focus

Everything fits on one little device. No accounts, no clutter.

01

Focus cycles

25 minutes of focus, 5 of rest. Click the digits to set whatever length works for you.

02

A cat that plays

It naps, wakes up, stretches and chases your cursor like a toy ball around the screen.

03

Focus music

Gamma beats, lofi and rain built in. Skip tracks and set the volume right on the device.

04

Task paper

Write what you're working on. The paper slides away as your session runs out.
